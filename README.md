## Long Form

**Articles from your chosen sources, organized by AI and delivered to your Kindle.**

Long Form is a live beta for deliberate reading. Readers choose their publications and customize an AI editor; the system assembles complete articles into a daily EPUB with a cover and linked contents. It combines a web app, authenticated MCP access, scheduled processing, and retry-safe delivery.
